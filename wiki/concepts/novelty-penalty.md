## What It Is

Evaluators that treat unfamiliar structures, unexpected tonal choices, or unconventional approaches as errors rather than as creative risk. Systematically conservative.

## Why It Matters

The most interesting artifacts are often the ones that don't fit the expected template. An evaluator calibrated on 'normal good' will miss 'surprisingly good.'

## Correction Strategy

Distinguish between incoherent and unexpected. Penalize the former, investigate the latter.
