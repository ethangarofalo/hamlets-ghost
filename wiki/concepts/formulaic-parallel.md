# Formulaic Parallel Construction

- tag_id: formulaic_parallel

## What It Is

Stacked parallel phrases where each element maps one domain onto another in the same grammatical frame. Looks like craft but is assembly-line rhetoric.

## Why It Matters

The parallel structure does the thinking for the reader. Each pair clicks into place too neatly. One strong observation is enough if it's the right one.

## Correction Strategy

Commit to one strong observation. Let it breathe.

## Examples of the Failure

- "turns his paralysis into a moral system, his fear into a philosophy"
- "where others see failure he finds vindication, where they see weakness he builds strength"

## Examples of the Fix

- "His paralysis becomes a moral system."
