# The Reframe Pivot

- tag_id: reframe_pivot

- provenance: seeded

## What It Is

Claims to reveal what something is really about by negating the obvious reading and substituting a deeper one. A formula for appearing insightful.

## Why It Matters

The 'real X' construction promises depth but delivers a pivot the reader can see coming by the third word.

## Correction Strategy

State the deeper reading directly. If it's true, it doesn't need theatrical setup.

## Examples of the Failure

- "The real tragedy isn't his death — it's that he saw it coming."
- "What matters here isn't the betrayal — it's the silence that followed."

## Examples of the Fix

- "He saw it coming."
