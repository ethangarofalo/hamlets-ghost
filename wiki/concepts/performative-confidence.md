# Performative Confidence

- tag_id: performative_confidence
- domain: anti-pattern
- provenance: seeded
- evidence_count: 0
- confidence: low
- status: tentative
- source: lab_observation
- detection_difficulty: medium

## What It Is

Business-lane outputs that project certainty without evidence. Assertions about market position, user needs, or strategic direction that sound authoritative but rest on nothing specific.

## Why It Matters

Trustworthy business writing earns confidence through specificity. Performed confidence reads as empty to anyone who knows the domain.

## Correction Strategy

Replace assertions with evidence. If the evidence doesn't exist, say so.

## Examples of the Failure

- "Our unique approach positions us perfectly to capture this market."
- "Users are increasingly demanding exactly this kind of solution."

## Examples of the Fix

- "Three of our five pilot customers renewed without a sales call."
