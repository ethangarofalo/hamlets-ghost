## What It Is

Business writing that earns trust through candor, specificity, and restraint rather than through polish and reassurance. Acknowledges limits. Names specifics. Does not oversell.

## Why It Matters

Trust is built by saying less than you could, not more than you should. The reader trusts the writer who acknowledges what they don't know.
