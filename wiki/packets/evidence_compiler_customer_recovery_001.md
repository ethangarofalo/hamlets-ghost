Dear subscriber,

Tonight’s performance of THE GLASS ORCHARD is cancelled. Our lead actor was injured in rehearsal this afternoon and, after a medical check, cannot perform safely.

The plain fact: we will not put the company or the audience in a room and hope for the best. That is not how we work, and it is not fair to you.

Your ticket will be refunded to the original payment method within 5–7 business days. If you’d rather transfer your ticket to next week’s performance, reply to this email with the word TRANSFER and we’ll handle it directly.

I know this is a letdown. We’re a small theatre, and you support us with more than money — you give us room to make work at all. Thank you for that trust, and for your patience tonight.

With respect,
Mara Ellison
Director, Lantern Box Theatre

Subject: Tonight’s performance

Dear subscribers,

I’m writing to the people who keep our small theatre alive: you, the subscribers who show up, bring friends, and make this place feel like a company rather than a room with seats.

We have to cancel tonight’s performance of A Midsummer Night’s Dream. Our lead actor was injured in rehearsal this afternoon and, after speaking with the company and our medical contact, we cannot safely stage the show as planned.

This is a practical decision, not a permanent one. We want to protect the actor’s recovery and return with a performance that meets the standard you deserve.

Next step: if you hold a ticket, reply to this email or call the box office by 5 p.m. tomorrow for a full refund or an exchange to any future date.

Thank you for your patience, and for staying with us when the work gets difficult.

Warmly,

Mara Ellison
Director, Lantern Theatre
