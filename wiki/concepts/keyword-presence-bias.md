## What It Is

Evaluators that check for explicit mention of assigned concepts rather than atmospheric or implied presence. Penalizes showing-not-telling. Rewards literal compliance over craft.

## Why It Matters

The best creative artifacts often convey their subject without naming it. An evaluator that demands the word 'ghost' will fail on the best ghost stories.

## Correction Strategy

Evaluate semantic presence, not string presence. Does the concept inhabit the text even if the word doesn't?
