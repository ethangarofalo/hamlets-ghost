# Generic Reassurance

## What It Is

Business-lane outputs that soothe rather than inform. Retention emails, apology copy, and strategy memos that could apply to any company because they say nothing specific to this one.

## Why It Matters

Reassurance without specificity reads as form letter. The reader recognizes the template.

## Correction Strategy

Name the specific situation, the specific loss, the specific remedy. If it could be sent by any company, rewrite it.

## Examples of the Failure

- "We value your feedback and are committed to continuous improvement."
- "We understand your frustration and want you to know we're working on it."

## Examples of the Fix

- "The export bug that hit your Thursday report is fixed. Here's what happened."
