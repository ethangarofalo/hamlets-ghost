# The Antithetical Formula

- tag_id: antithetical_formula

- provenance: seeded

## What It Is

Any sentence where the first clause negates or sets up a reframe that the second clause delivers. The construction pre-digests the insight for the reader instead of letting juxtaposition do the work.

## Why It Matters

Hands the reader the turn rather than letting them discover it. Real prose lets the juxtaposition speak without scaffolding.

## Correction Strategy

Place the two ideas next to each other and trust the reader.

## Examples of the Failure

- "He doesn't just avoid the game — he builds an entire theology around refusal."
- "This isn't about politics — it's about the stories we tell ourselves."

## Examples of the Fix

- "He builds an entire theology around refusal."
