## What It Is

Signals depth without committing to a specific observation. The adjective slot can be filled with almost anything, which is the tell — if the frame works with any adjective, the frame is doing the work, not the thought.

## Why It Matters

The construction gestures at meaning without producing it. It's a placeholder for insight.

## Correction Strategy

Name the specific thing. Replace the hedge with the observation itself.
