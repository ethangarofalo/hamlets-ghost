# Coherence Over Life

## What It Is

Evaluators that reward structural coherence and safety over vividness, risk, and emotional pressure. Produces a systematic preference for competent-but-flat over alive-but-uneven.

## Why It Matters

Coherence is necessary but not sufficient. An artifact can be perfectly coherent and completely forgettable. Human reviewers often prefer the artifact with more life, even if it's rougher.

## Correction Strategy

When scoring, weight vividness and memorability alongside coherence. A safe artifact should not automatically outscore a vivid one.
