# Stacked Poetic Devices

- tag_id: stacked_poetic_devices

- provenance: seeded

## What It Is

Multiple rhetorical devices layered into a single sentence, producing something that reads like prize prose but says nothing precise.

## Why It Matters

Poetic density without earned context. Each device competes for attention. The sentence becomes about its own cleverness rather than its subject.

## Correction Strategy

One device per sentence, chosen because it reveals something.

## Examples of the Failure

- "wear their weakness like a scar they refuse to powder over"
- "the silence between them grew teeth and learned to bite"

## Examples of the Fix

- "He won't hide the weakness."
